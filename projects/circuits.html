Project 03 / Electronics

Analogue Circuit Design

Designing circuits to a specification, choosing components through calculation and checking what changes when a simulated circuit becomes a physical one.

Role

Team leader · Two-person team

Period

November–December 2024

Tools

Multisim · Oscilloscope · Multimeter

Focus

BJT & operational amplifier circuits

Design → Simulate → Measure · Illustrative sketch

An illustrative amplifier sketch, rather than an exact schematic of the tested circuit.

01 / The brief02 / Design & test03 / What I learned

01 / The brief

Design against requirements.

This two-person laboratory project explored BJT and operational amplifier circuits, including transistor switching, common-emitter amplification, adjustable-gain amplifiers, a low-pass filter and a Wien bridge oscillator.

My work focused on experimental design, component selection, parameter calculations, simulation, debugging and data recording. My teammate handled the breadboard wiring during the build stage.

02 / Design & test

A calculation is the starting point.

01 / Calculate

Select suitable components.

I reviewed datasheets and calculated circuit parameters against the experiment requirements, checking the chosen devices and component values.

02 / Simulate

Check the expected response.

I used Multisim to verify circuit feasibility and examine operating bias, gain and frequency response before physical testing.

03 / Measure

Inspect the real circuit.

I used an oscilloscope and multimeter to test assembled circuits, recording measurements for comparison with theory and simulation.

04 / Investigate

Find the discrepancy.

I helped identify and correct faults such as reversed component polarity and abnormal bias voltages, then checked the resulting circuit behaviour.

03 / What I learned

Use the model, then question it.

The practical value of simulation became clearer when the measured circuit differed from the expected response. Checking component connections and operating conditions helped connect the circuit model to the behaviour seen on the bench.

The experience developed my approach to component selection and fault investigation—skills I can carry into robotics hardware integration.
