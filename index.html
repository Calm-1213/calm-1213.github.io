Undergraduate dissertation · Individual project · 2025–2026

Test Rig for Soft Fluidic Actuators

I designed and built an adjustable rig to measure the peak blocked force of PneuNet and fibre-reinforced soft actuators.

  • Integrated mechanical fixtures, a manual pneumatic supply and force measurement.
  • Investigated leaks and unstable sensing, then revised the setup.
  • Tested two actuator types at six air volumes each.
