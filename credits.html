Soft actuator project

Technical descriptions and measurements are drawn from Yuzhou Li’s undergraduate dissertation, Test rig for soft fluidic actuators (2026), supervised by Dr Nicolas Herzig at the Sussex Artificial Intelligence Institute, Zhejiang Gongshang University. The chart and downloadable CSV reproduce the rounded values in Tables 4–5.

Actuator fabrication in the dissertation was informed by these resources:

  • Soft Robotics Toolkit — PneuNets Bending Actuator
  • Soft Robotics Toolkit — Fibre-reinforced Actuator Cardboard Mould

No literature photographs or diagrams from the dissertation have been reproduced here. The circuit illustration is a schematic visual made for this site and is labelled as illustrative.

Measurement scope

The actuator rig used manual volume-controlled inflation, without direct pressure measurement. Its force–volume datasets demonstrate the test platform and should not be treated as a controlled comparison of actuator designs. The 60 g scale check was a basic verification, not full calibration.

The homepage portrait was enhanced from a photograph supplied by Yuzhou Li using AI-assisted image restoration.
